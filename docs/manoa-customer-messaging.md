# Manoa Customer Messaging

## One-line positioning

Manoa is your calendar assistant by text.

## Short product description

Manoa helps you manage your calendar by text instead of opening your calendar app.
You can schedule something new, move or cancel plans, add events from screenshots and flyers, and get agenda and reminder texts.

## The clearest feature list

Use this version when someone needs to understand Manoa fast:

- Schedule by text
- Move or cancel plans by text
- Add events from screenshots, photos, and flyers
- Get agenda and reminder texts
- Works with Google Calendar, Outlook, and Apple Calendar

## What Manoa does today

Use this version when someone wants a little more detail:

- Check your schedule for today, tomorrow, or next week
- Book new events by text
- Suggest open times and let you reply with `1`, `2`, or `3`
- Reschedule or cancel calendar events
- Add events from screenshots, reminder cards, invites, flyers, and school handouts
- Route events to the right calendar across work, home, and family calendars
- Send morning agenda texts
- Send reminder texts before timed events

## Messaging guardrails

Keep the story simple.
Lead with the four core jobs:

1. schedule
2. move or cancel
3. add from photos
4. reminders and agenda

Do not lead with edge cases, integrations, or setup details unless someone asks.
