# Manoa Growth Plan

## Goal

Reach **2,000 paying users within 6 months** of real launch.

That is ambitious, but it is not absurd. It only becomes unrealistic if we try to
grow before the product is dependable, clear, and easy to talk about.

## Reality Check

2,000 users in 6 months means roughly:

- **333 new paying users per month**
- **77 new paying users per week**
- **11 new paying users per day**

That is very doable if Manoa gets strong word of mouth inside a few good niches.

It is not a "launch once and wait" number. It is a:

- tight onboarding
- strong referral loop
- clear positioning
- weekly content and outreach
- fast product iteration

number.

## What Has To Be True Before We Push Hard

### 1. The first-user flow feels complete

A new user should be able to:

1. sign up
2. pay
3. connect a calendar
4. land on the dashboard
5. know what number to text
6. send one first successful text

If any of that feels confusing, traffic will leak.

### 2. The product has one clear promise

The promise should stay simple:

**Stop scheduling. Just text Manoa.**

People should understand the product in under 10 seconds:

- book meetings by text
- get reminders by text
- manage your calendar by text

### 3. The trust pieces are in place

Before real promotion:

- Twilio SMS campaign approved
- Google app published and clean
- Outlook flow tested well enough to stand behind it
- billing and cancel flow feel trustworthy
- privacy policy and terms are stable

### 4. We can measure the funnel

We should know:

- visitors
- signup starts
- paid conversions
- calendar connects
- first text sent
- 7-day retained users
- canceled users

If we cannot see where people drop, growth will feel random.

## Best Users To Win First

Do not market to "everyone with a calendar."

Win people who already feel scheduling pain every week:

### 1. Busy solo operators

- consultants
- coaches
- recruiters
- freelancers
- small agency owners

### 2. People juggling personal + work calendars

- professionals with a work life and family life
- people using multiple calendars already

### 3. High-volume schedulers

- real estate
- sales
- operations
- founders
- assistants who are doing too much manually

These people are more likely to immediately feel:

"Oh, I would actually use this."

## What I Would Focus On In The Product Right Now

### Must-have before real push

- first text success should feel easy
- dashboard should be crystal clear
- save contact should work smoothly
- calendar routing should feel safe
- login should never feel flaky
- subscription state should always be accurate

### Next product wins that help growth

- referral prompt after first successful week
- "share Manoa with a friend" flow
- default duration and preferences memory
- stronger onboarding examples by use case
- better handling of tentative invites and calendar ambiguity

### What I would not prioritize yet

- Apple calendar
- team admin features
- mobile app
- complex analytics dashboards
- broad paid ads before retention is proven

## 6-Month Plan

## Phase 1: Finish Launch Readiness

**Window:** now through SMS approval + final verification

### Goal

Be ready to test with real people the moment texting is fully live.

### Success criteria

- product works end to end
- onboarding is polished
- support issues are understandable
- homepage explains the value clearly
- dashboard feels trustworthy

### Tasks

- finish final onboarding polish
- test Google thoroughly
- test Outlook carefully
- make sure billing, cancel, reconnect, and disconnect all work
- add basic funnel tracking
- create a short founder demo video
- collect 5 to 10 early testers the moment texting is live

### Target

**10 to 25 real beta users**

## Phase 2: Soft Launch To Tight Niches

**Window:** month 1

### Goal

Get early paying users from direct contact and communities.

### Channels

- founder network
- friends of friends
- niche Slack groups
- Reddit where appropriate
- LinkedIn posts
- personal outreach

### Positioning

Lead with one sentence:

**Text Manoa instead of opening your calendar.**

Then show one example.

### Target

**100 paying users**

## Phase 3: Build Repeatable Demand

**Window:** months 2 to 3

### Goal

Start getting steady inbound interest.

### Channels

- SEO pages around real use cases
- short product demos
- customer screenshots and stories
- simple referral asks
- review and testimonial collection

### Content plan

Publish practical pages around problems people already search for:

- schedule by text
- calendar reminders by text
- reschedule appointments by text
- multiple calendars by text
- Google Calendar assistant
- Outlook calendar assistant
- recurring meetings by text
- assistant for busy professionals

### Target

**250 to 400 paying users**

## Phase 4: Turn Retention Into Growth

**Window:** months 3 to 4

### Goal

Make users bring in more users.

### Product moves

- ask for a referral after a user books or reschedules several times
- add simple "share Manoa" copy in the dashboard
- highlight "save 30 minutes a week" style outcomes
- collect testimonial quotes from delighted users

### Marketing moves

- founder-led content
- "watch me text my calendar" demos
- landing pages tailored to 2 or 3 best niches

### Target

**600 to 800 paying users**

## Phase 5: Double Down On What Works

**Window:** months 4 to 5

### Goal

Scale the channels already showing conversion.

### What to do

- keep the best-performing use-case pages
- cut weak channels quickly
- start small paid experiments only if retention is healthy
- push testimonials and case studies harder
- tighten onboarding based on support questions

### Target

**1,100 to 1,300 paying users**

## Phase 6: Reach 2,000

**Window:** months 5 to 6

### Goal

Make Manoa feel established enough that people trust it quickly.

### What matters most

- visible social proof
- simple website
- fewer support issues
- stronger referral loop
- better conversion on homepage and dashboard

### Target

**2,000 paying users**

## Weekly Operating Rhythm

If we want 2,000 users, we need a weekly rhythm, not random bursts.

Every week:

- ship **1 product improvement**
- publish **2 useful pieces of content**
- do **50 direct outreach messages** to likely users or connectors
- talk to **5 users**
- review the funnel
- keep only the channels that are working

## Metrics To Watch

The important numbers:

- homepage visitor -> signup start
- signup start -> paid
- paid -> calendar connected
- calendar connected -> first text sent
- first text sent -> week-1 retained
- monthly churn
- referrals per active user

If any step is weak, fix that before trying to add more traffic.

## My Honest Bet On How Manoa Gets There

I do **not** think Manoa gets to 2,000 users from SEO alone.

I think it gets there from a combination of:

- a sharp homepage
- use-case SEO pages
- direct founder-led outreach
- word of mouth
- people showing it to coworkers or friends
- simple product delight once the first text works

In other words:

**the website gets attention**
and
**the product gets people to stay and share it**

## The Right Way To Think About The Number

Do not think:

"I need 2,000 people."

Think:

"I need the first 25, then 100, then 300, then 1,000."

That is how this becomes manageable.

## Bottom Line

Yes, Manoa has real potential.

And yes, **2,000 users in 6 months is possible**.

But the path is not "launch and hope."

The path is:

- make the first experience feel excellent
- pick the right early users
- learn fast
- keep shipping every week
- build word of mouth on top of reliability
